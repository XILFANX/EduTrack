# Getting Started with EduTrack

Welcome to EduTrack! Whether you are a Principal, Teacher, or Parent, your dashboard provides exactly what you need.

---

## Step 1: Claim Your Account

EduTrack uses an invite-only onboarding system to ensure you are linked to the correct school and students. You cannot sign up from the homepage blindly.

1. You will receive an **Invite Link** via WhatsApp, SMS, or Email from your school administrator or class teacher.
2. Click the link.
3. Enter your name and set a secure password.
4. Click **Complete Setup**.

You will immediately be logged in and redirected to your specific portal.

> **Note:** If you are a Parent with multiple children at the same school, your single account will automatically show all your children's profiles.

## Step 2: Navigate Your Portal

Depending on your role, your experience will look different.

### For Principals & Admins
Your portal is at `/dashboard`. You have full oversight of school operations.
- See active student counts, fee collection summaries, and overall attendance.
- Navigate to the **Classes**, **Teachers**, or **Finances** tabs on the left to drill down.

### For Teachers
Your portal is at `/teacher`.
- **Class Teachers:** You will see a list of your assigned students. You can mark daily attendance and generate invite links for parents.
- **Subject Teachers:** You will see the specific subjects you teach (e.g., Grade 4 Math) and can enter exam scores.

### For Parents
Your portal is at `/parent`. 
- View your child's recent exam results.
- Check their termly fee balance.
- Pay school fees directly from your phone.

[SCREENSHOT: /parent/dashboard — the parent view showing a child's fee balance and recent grades]

---

## Step 3: Install the App (Web App)

EduTrack is designed to work perfectly on your phone without needing to visit the App Store.

**On iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open your dashboard in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

**On Android (Chrome):**
1. Open your dashboard in Chrome.
2. Tap the **Menu** (three dots) in the top right.
3. Tap **Install App** or **Add to Home screen**.

Now you can open EduTrack just like a normal app on your phone.
