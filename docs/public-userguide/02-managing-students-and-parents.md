# Managing Students & Parents

**Goal:** Enroll students into classes and grant their parents access to the Parent Portal.

*This guide is typically used by Class Teachers and Principals.*

---

## 1. Adding a Student

Students are always assigned to a specific Class.

1. Navigate to **Classes**.
2. Open the Class you want to add students to (e.g., "Grade 4").
3. Click **Add Student**.
4. Enter the student's Full Name, Admission Number, and Date of Birth.
5. Click **Save**.

The student is now enrolled.

### Bulk Import
If you have a large spreadsheet of students, you can use the **Import Students** button inside the class. Download the CSV template, fill it out, and upload it.

---

## 2. Inviting Parents to the Portal

Once a student is in the system, you need to invite their parent. EduTrack does not require you to manually create parent accounts. Instead, you send them an invite link.

1. Go to the student's profile.
2. Click **Generate Parent Invite**.
3. Send this link to the parent via WhatsApp or SMS.

When the parent clicks the link, they will be prompted to create an account. They are immediately and securely linked to that specific student.

> **Note:** If a parent has multiple children in the school, they should click the invite link for *each* child using the same email address. The system will merge the children under their single Parent Portal.

---

## 3. What Parents Can See

Once a parent is linked, they get access to the `/parent` portal. 

They can view:
- **Academics:** Report cards, recent exam scores, and attendance records.
- **Finances:** Current fee balances, historic invoices, and payment receipts.
- **Communications:** Direct messages from teachers or the principal.

Parents **cannot** see data belonging to any other student.

---

## 4. Transferring or Graduating Students

At the end of the academic year, you will need to move students to the next grade.

1. Navigate to **Students**.
2. Select the checkboxes next to the students you wish to move.
3. Click **Transfer Class** (at the top of the table).
4. Select the destination class and confirm.

If a student leaves the school, open their profile and click **Deactivate**. This preserves their historical academic and financial records but removes them from active class rosters and stops fee invoices from generating.
